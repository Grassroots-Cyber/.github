## Grassroots Cyber Foundation

Grassroots Cyber Foundation (GCF) is a Texas-based nonprofit for people getting started in cybersecurity. We empower the next generation of cybersecurity professionals through education, mentorship, scholarships and community-driven opportunities.

Newcomers to cybersecurity often lack clear guidance, hands-on practice, professional connections, or the money for training and certification. GCF is building ways in through two programs:

- **[Ginger Hacker Initiative (GHI)](https://gingerhacker.org/)**: education and workforce development.
- **[Noob Village](https://www.noobvillage.org/)**: community events and welcoming, beginner-friendly spaces to learn, ask questions and meet the cybersecurity community.

Learn more at **[grassrootscyber.org](https://grassrootscyber.org)**.
